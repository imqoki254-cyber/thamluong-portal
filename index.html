METHODOLOGICAL RIGOR & SCIENTIFIC GUARDRAILS
Usable vs. Inaccessible Green: Network mapping filters out fenced vegetation so only shade along walkable paths is counted.
Water Cooling Neutrality: Canal-water thermal parameters are varied explicitly in sensitivity runs rather than assuming automatic cooling.
Mixed-Effects & Grouped Validation: Repeated sensor observations within sites/days are modelled via mixed-effects, avoiding pseudoreplication.
Uncertainty Thresholds: Scenario differences smaller than instrument/model RMSE will not be claimed as established thermal improvements.

Analytical Scope & Operational Boundaries

The core empirical study comprises 3 mapped and monitored reaches (12 stations) and high-resolution scenario evaluation in 2 representative reaches.

Explicitly Outside Core Scope:
  • Full-corridor CFD simulation at sub-meter grid
  • Clinical or epidemiological health outcome tracking
  • Chemical air pollutant dispersion modelling
  • Black-box ML without independent validation data
Research Ethics & Open Science
  • No Identifiable Human Data: Core physical monitoring requires no personal data.
  • Privacy in Fieldwork: Site documentation photographs strictly avoid identifiable faces.
  • Transparent Limitations: Limited seasonal sampling and hypothetical scenario status stated in all conclusions.
